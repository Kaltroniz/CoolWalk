          How it works, and the limits

          Where the data comes from: Surface temperature from USGS Landsat 8 (June 2026), vegetation index from Copernicus Sentinel-2 (June 2026), and walkable street geometry from OpenStreetMap.

          How routes are found: Street segments hold a heat cost based on high surface temperature and lack of greenery. An A* router searches for the coolest path, capped at a sensible detour above the direct route.

          The limits: Satellite observations capture surface temperature on clear summer mornings, not ambient air temperature or transient humidity. Shading from trees and awnings is reflected in vegetation and temperature proxies, but tall building shadows vary by time of day. Walking pace is assumed at 1.35 m/s.
