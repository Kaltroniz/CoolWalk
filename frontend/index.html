          ℹ How It Works & Scientific Limits

          Data sources: Surface temperature from USGS Landsat 8 (June 2026), vegetation index from Copernicus Sentinel-2 (June 2026), and pedestrian network from OpenStreetMap.

          Routing algorithm: Street segments carry a heat cost weighted by high surface temperatures and low greenery. An A* router searches for the coolest path within a bounded detour cap.

          Limits: Satellites measure radiative skin temperature on clear summer mornings, not ambient 2m air temperature or transient humidity. Tree canopy is captured via NDVI, while building shadows vary by hour. Walking pace: 1.35 m/s.

          Heat is estimated from Landsat surface temperature and Sentinel-2 vegetation. It shows where streets hold heat, not the air temperature you will feel. Download the data (GeoJSON).
